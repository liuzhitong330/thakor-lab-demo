The Thakor Lab combines image-guided intervention, focused ultrasound and translational biology. The advertised neural-interface role extends that precision-delivery logic to periportal autonomic targets, where synchronized stimulation and physiological data have to support a defensible experimental call.

I built a small analysis over public, animal-level hepatoportal pFUS clamp data. It pairs each swine's pFUS and sham time courses, checks glucose stability and missingness, and turns adjustable rules into a transparent review queue rather than treating every apparent response as equally reliable.

How it works

The source workbook reports glucose infusion rate (GIR) and blood glucose as percentages of each swine's baseline before and after time zero, when either pFUS or sham was applied. For a selected animal, the explorer overlays the paired traces and evaluates the 30–90 minute window. A candidate response must clear a GIR-uplift threshold, remain within a chosen mean glucose-deviation limit, and retain at least 80% of expected samples.

Analysis 2 — Ask whether the direction survives model changes

Result
Late-window GIR is higher with pFUS in both rat clamp models
Mean animal-level GIR, 60–120 min (mg kg⁻¹ min⁻¹)

Across the independently processed animal traces, DIO rats averaged 11.55 under pFUS versus 4.59 under sham, a difference of 6.95 mg kg⁻¹ min⁻¹. In the T1D model the corresponding means were 39.16 and 26.79, a difference of 12.37 mg kg⁻¹ min⁻¹. This does not establish a causal mechanism, but it supports carrying the response direction into a tightly synchronized periportal experiment with prespecified controls.

Why this matters for the lab

Periportal neuromodulation produces several clocks at once: stimulation onset, neural activity, vascular signals and metabolic readouts. An animal-level audit makes timing, missing data and physiological stability visible before traces are pooled. The same framework can compare endovascular, ultrasound, electrophysiological or thermal perturbations without pretending that their signals are interchangeable.

What I would bring

I would bring R, Python and MATLAB analysis experience, an independent computational-neurostimulation project, and practical research habits from animal-model, IVIS, assay, QC and documentation work. I have not independently run focused-ultrasound or electrophysiology acquisitions; the value shown here is data integration and experimental reasoning that can support hands-on training.

Methods note

Artifact: APPLICATION_DEMO. The site uses the authors' animal-level processed values in SD for Fig. 3, mirrored by BioStudies S-EPMC10127248, from Cotero et al. 2022. The 30–90 min and 60–120 min summaries were independently calculated from non-missing values. “Pass” is an exploratory review label using adjustable descriptive thresholds; it is not a reported endpoint, inferential test or claim of clinical efficacy. The workbook does not expose raw acquisition clocks, waveforms or targeting logs, so this demo cannot test clock drift, stimulus artifacts or anatomical accuracy. The Thakor Lab profile informed the role-specific framing; no verified target-owned public GitHub repository was found in the lab, PI, publication or GitHub searches completed for this project.
